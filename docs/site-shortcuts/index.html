Site shortcuts: your home, your sites, your order

Tubyo's home gathers your video sites as shortcuts. You choose what appears, in which order, and you can add any other site.

Adding a site

Add a site from the + button on the home screen or from Settings. Tubyo recognises YouTube, YouTube Music, Twitch, TikTok and Instagram automatically, and accepts any other compatible video site.

Reorder and hide

Drag the handles to change the order, hide shortcuts you do not need and restore the default home in one tap. Hidden sites stay reachable from the library.

Child mode too

In child mode the home only shows YouTube Kids and Kidoodle.TV: no shortcut to your sites, and no Tubyo ads.
