Tubyo›All your video sites

YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram: one app for your videos

Instead of an app per platform, Tubyo brings your video sites together in a browser built for video: shortcuts, tabs, a creator library and resume.

Supported platforms

YouTube, YouTube Music, Twitch, TikTok and Instagram are recognised automatically, with their logos and colours. Any other video site can be added as a custom shortcut.

Why go through Tubyo

You keep your browsing habits and gain native PiP, free ad filtering, a creator library, resume, background playback and a protected child mode.

No account, no data collection

Tubyo needs no account and does not sit between you and the platforms: you stay signed in to your sites, and the library stays on your device.
