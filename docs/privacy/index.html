Privacy policy

This policy describes Tubyo for iPhone and iPad and this support website. Contact the Tubyo developer at elm.dev.code@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Data on your device

Tubyo does not require a Tubyo account. Saved content, creators, playback progress, imported files and preferences are managed locally. The parental code is protected using the system Keychain. Website cookies and sign-ins are stored by the embedded browser. Device backups may retain app data according to your Apple settings.

Websites and video services

Opening a website, playing media or loading a thumbnail connects your device to that provider. Providers can receive your IP address, browser information and any information you give them, and may use cookies under their own policies. Tubyo is not an anonymizing network. Saved links are not downloaded copies of website videos.

Advertising and consent

Normal mode uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s consent tools for Tubyo advertisements. Tubyo requests non-personalized advertising. This does not mean no data processing: Google may process IP addresses, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, performance information and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Required consent choices are presented before eligible ad requests. Available privacy options can be revisited in the app’s settings.

Child mode does not request or display Tubyo ads. Websites opened in child mode remain separate services with their own privacy practices. Built-in filtering does not guarantee that every third-party request or advertisement is removed.

Google Privacy Policy · Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

Purchases and casting

Apple processes subscriptions and payments. Tubyo uses verified StoreKit purchase status to enable ad removal and does not receive your card or banking details. If you use casting, local-network discovery and the selected media information are used to connect to your receiver; the receiver and its provider may process connection information under their policies.

Support and this website

If you email support, your address and the information you provide are processed to handle your request. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. This website uses no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and records visitor IP addresses for security. Email is handled through Gmail. Their privacy policies apply to their services.

Retention and your choices

Manage or delete saved items, history and browser data using the app controls. Deleting the app removes its ordinary local container, while backups and Keychain data can be managed separately through your device and Apple settings. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Providers set their own retention periods.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact support for data handled by Tubyo; contact the relevant provider for website, advertising or payment data they control. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Changes

We will update this page when data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes requiring consent will be presented through the applicable app or provider flow.
