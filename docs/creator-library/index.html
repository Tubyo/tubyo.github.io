Creator library: everything you follow, in one place

Tubyo keeps your videos, creators, collections and resume list in a space of your own, with no account to create and nothing uploaded to a server.

Videos and creators

Save a video from any supported site, add a creator from their page, then filter by platform (YouTube, YouTube Music, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram) or by collection.

Resume and finish

Resume keeps the exact position of every video. In-progress and finished videos each have their section, and you decide what stays in the library.

Your data stays with you

The library lives on your device. Exporting links and collections is manual, and browsing history is excluded from cloud backup.
