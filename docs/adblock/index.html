Ad filtering: fewer interruptions on your video sites

Tubyo includes free ad filtering for the websites you open. The goal is simple: give you the video, not the breaks before it starts.

What Tubyo filters

Filtering acts on compatible ads on the web pages you watch, including breaks inserted before a video starts on supported sites. Nothing to enable: it is built in and free.

What Tubyo does not block

Tubyo does not remove the ads platforms serve inside their own players (YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram). No third-party app can do that reliably and permanently, and Tubyo does not claim to.

Free, without a subscription

Filtering never depends on a subscription. The optional Tubyo subscription only removes the ads Tubyo itself shows (one site opening and one home banner); website filtering stays free either way.
