Chromecast: send your video to the TV

From a page’s action sheet, Tubyo offers the native Chromecast picker. The TV receives the media link and connects to its source itself.

How to cast

Open a compatible page or media, tap Chromecast in the page actions, then pick your device in the system picker. Playback continues on the TV while you keep using your phone.

What is shared

The receiver gets the media link and connects straight to its source. Your browser cookies are not sent to it, and Tubyo disables the Cast SDK’s optional analytics logging.

Requirements

A compatible cast device on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone. The picker comes from the Android system; which sources work depends on the receiver, as with any casting.
