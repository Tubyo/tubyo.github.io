Child mode: a separate video space, protected by a code

Tubyo’s child mode keeps children’s videos in their own space, with their own library and viewing history. You stay in control through the parental code.

A genuinely separate space

YouTube Kids and Kidoodle.TV open in a dedicated session: a child space with its own library, creators and resume list, never mixed with yours.

Protection and session length

A parental code protects leaving child mode, you choose a session length, and controls can be locked during playback to avoid accidental taps.

No Tubyo ads

No Tubyo ads are shown in child mode, even without a subscription. Website filtering stays active on compatible sites.
