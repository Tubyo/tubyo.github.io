Private browsing: watch without leaving a trace

Tubyo asks for no account. Private browsing opens an isolated WebView profile with no shared cookies, and its history never joins your library.

What private browsing does

A private session uses an isolated profile: no cookies shared with your normal browsing, no history entry, and the profile is deleted when you close it.

Your data stays local

The library lives on the device. Browsing history and watch positions are excluded from cloud backup, and exporting your links stays manual.

No account, anywhere

No sign-up, no Tubyo profile, no sync: you stay signed in to your own sites, and Tubyo does not collect your activity to resell it.

A few good answers.

Can I use Tubyo without an account?+

Yes, there is no Tubyo account at all: everything works locally from the moment you install it.

Does private browsing disable PiP?+

PiP stays available; a private session is simply excluded from background playback and history.
