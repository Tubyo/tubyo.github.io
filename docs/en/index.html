Picture in Picture on iPhone and iPad

Start a video and leave the app: Picture in Picture keeps it playing in a floating window, and background audio continues for supported sources. You choose between the website player and the Apple player.

What Tubyo’s ad filtering does

Tubyo filters compatible ads on the websites you open, including breaks inserted before a video starts. It does not block the ads YouTube, Twitch or TikTok insert inside their own players, and effectiveness varies by website and country.

The subscription is optional

An ad may appear when a website opens, alongside a banner on the home screen. Tubyo adds no ad breaks during your videos. Subscribing removes only these Tubyo ads; website ad filtering stays free.

A few good answers.

Do I pay for ad filtering or PiP?+

No. Every feature is free. The optional subscription only removes ads displayed by Tubyo.

Can I keep subtitles and quality controls?+

The website player keeps its available controls. You can also choose the Apple player for supported sources.

Is child mode free?+

Yes, including its separate library, parental code and session limit. It never shows Tubyo ads, even without a subscription.

When can I download Tubyo?+

The iPhone and iPad release is being prepared for the App Store. Android is planned next. The download link will be added when the app is available.

Does Tubyo block YouTube ads?+

No. Tubyo filters compatible ads on the websites you open; it does not remove the ads platforms insert inside their own players. Every feature stays free and child mode never shows Tubyo ads.

Does Picture in Picture work on iPhone and iPad?+

Yes, on supported, up-to-date devices: the video keeps playing in a floating window while you use another app. The controls available depend on the website and the system.

Practical guides

Picture in Picture

PiP keeps the video in a small floating window while you do something else on your phone. Tubyo starts it from the video sites you already use, with no complicated setup.

↗

Ad filtering

Tubyo includes free ad filtering for the websites you open. The goal is simple: give you the video, not the breaks before it starts.

↗

Chromecast

From a page’s action sheet, Tubyo offers the native Chromecast picker. The TV receives the media link and connects to its source itself.

↗

Background playback

For music videos and supported sources, Tubyo keeps the audio running: lock your phone, switch apps or listen while walking.

↗

Full screen

Tubyo's full screen uses the website's own controls to stay compatible: quality, subtitles and playback speed stay those your video site offers.

↗

Live video

Tubyo opens Twitch and other live sites like any video site, with the extra video features: PiP, full screen, background audio and resume.

↗

Child mode

Tubyo’s child mode keeps children’s videos in their own space, with their own library and viewing history. You stay in control through the parental code.

↗

Creator library

Tubyo keeps your videos, creators, collections and resume list in a space of your own, with no account to create and nothing uploaded to a server.

↗

Save a video

From the page actions, Tubyo saves the current video or the page's creator. Everything lands in your library, on your device.

↗

Site shortcuts

Tubyo's home gathers your video sites as shortcuts. You choose what appears, in which order, and you can add any other site.

↗

All your video sites

Instead of an app per platform, Tubyo brings your video sites together in a browser built for video: shortcuts, tabs, a creator library and resume.

↗

Private browsing

Tubyo asks for no account. Private browsing opens an isolated WebView profile with no shared cookies, and its history never joins your library.

↗

Android, iPhone, iPad

Tubyo brings YouTube, YouTube Music, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram and your own sites into a single video space, free, on phone and tablet alike.
