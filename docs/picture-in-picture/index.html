Picture in Picture: your video keeps playing, so do you

PiP keeps the video in a small floating window while you do something else on your phone. Tubyo starts it from the video sites you already use, with no complicated setup.

What Picture in Picture does

Start a video on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok or another supported site and leave the app: it stays visible in a floating window you can move around. The sound continues while you read messages, take notes or switch apps.

Full screen and background audio

Full screen stays available for supported sources, on iPhone and iPad alike. For music videos the audio can keep playing in the background and from the lock screen, like a music app.

What can vary

Every website keeps its own player and its own rules: depending on the site, playback may pause, subtitles may differ or quality may be limited. PiP itself is always free in Tubyo.
