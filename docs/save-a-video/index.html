Saving a video: one gesture and it is yours

From the page actions, Tubyo saves the current video or the page's creator. Everything lands in your library, on your device.

Saving a video

Open the video and tap Save in library: Tubyo keeps the real title, the creator when it can be identified and the playback position. Tapping again simply tells you it is already there.

Adding the creator

Add a creator from any recognised platform. If the creator is already in your library, Tubyo says so instead of creating a duplicate.

Finding and organising

Filter by platform or collection, resume every video at the right moment and remove what you no longer need. Exporting your links and collections stays available in Settings.

A few good answers.

Where are my saved videos?+

In your local library, on your device. No Tubyo account is required.

Does it download the video?+

No: Tubyo stores the link and public metadata. Importing your own media files remains available.
